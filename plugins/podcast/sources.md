## How to use this list

1. Before mining a new show, check `ledger.py mined --show "<name>"`. If
   it has episodes recorded, extend it; do not restart from scratch.
2. Prefer "direct match" shows over "broader" ones, unless the goal is to
   find a contradiction to something already in the ledger.
3. After mining a show for the first time, add it to "Already mined" with
   a one-line note on its angle. The note is what makes the next run's
   source selection fast.
4. Record what a show covers, not a verdict on its host. A finding about
   what someone said belongs in the ledger with its episode citation.
